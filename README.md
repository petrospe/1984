# George Orwell - 1984 Wall Poster

A single-page HTML poster inspired by George Orwell’s novel, 1984, presenting the central themes of surveillance, state power, perpetual war, social control, and the manipulation of truth.

## Project Overview

This project is a stylized editorial poster designed as a visual summary of the book’s political ideas. It combines a dark minimalist aesthetic with a series of key messages from the novel, including:

- The slogan: “War is peace. Freedom is slavery. Ignorance is strength.”
- The political logic of perpetual conflict and manufactured scarcity
- The three-class system and the cyclical nature of power
- The role of the proletariat under strict social conditioning
- The use of distraction, entertainment, and digital surveillance as tools of control
- The idea that love and intimacy are political acts under authoritarian rule
- The party’s obsession with preserving power above all else

## File Structure

- `index.html` — complete poster page with embedded CSS and all content
- `README.md` — project description and usage notes

## How to View

Open `index.html` in a browser to view the poster.

For example:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit:

```text
http://localhost:8000/
```

## Design Notes

The page intentionally uses:

- A dark, high-contrast visual theme
- Monospace typography to evoke a dystopian, mechanical atmosphere
- Strong section headers and political slogans
- Print-friendly styling for poster-like output

## Themes Reflected in the Poster

The poster is built around Orwell’s central ideas, including:

1. Totalitarian control through propaganda and language
2. The manipulation of history and truth
3. Social obedience through distraction and entertainment
4. The destruction of private human connection
5. The permanence of power structures under changing rulers
